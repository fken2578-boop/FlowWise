Model assumptions

Water flows steadily through one full, straight pipe between open reservoirs. Total pump head equals the rise between the water surfaces plus pipe friction loss. The estimate omits fittings, entrance and exit losses, and pump curves.

The model uses water density of 1000 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s and constant combined pump and drive efficiency. Reynolds number and Darcy friction factor are dimensionless. In transitional flow (2300 ≤ Re < 4000), friction and energy estimates are unavailable.

Annual savings equal baseline electricity cost minus upgrade electricity cost. For positive savings, simple payback divides the extra installed cost by annual savings. It excludes maintenance, financing and discounting.
